
# Adwuma Track

**Run your business. Track every cedi.**

Adwuma Track is an offline-first business management application designed for Ghanaian traders and small business owners.

## Vision

To make business management simple, accessible and reliable for every trader.

## Core Features

- Business management
- Sales tracking
- Inventory management
- Customer and debtor management
- Expense tracking
- Financial reports
- Offline transactions
- Automatic data synchronization

## Technology Stack

- React Native
- Expo
- JavaScript
- Expo Router
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- AsyncStorage

## Target Market

Small business owners, shopkeepers, retailers and traders in Ghana.

## Currency

Ghana Cedi (GH₵)

## Development Status

Planning and foundation stage.

## Project Goal

Build a reliable, professional mobile business management platform.
